📖 Introduction to AI Waste Segregation

🌎 Why Automated Waste Segregation?

Waste mismanagement leads to pollution, landfill overflow, and environmental hazards. Over 91% of plastic waste goes unrecycled, highlighting the need for efficient waste classification and disposal.

🎯 Project Objectives

  • 🤖 Develop an AI-powered waste segregation system using a SCARA robot.
  • 📊 Optimize robotic trajectory planning with Genetic Algorithms (GA) and L-BFGS-B optimization.
  • 🎥 Achieve real-time waste classification using AI-based vision models.
  • ⚡ Ensure high energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness in sorting operations.

🛠️ How Does Our System Work?

  • 📸 AI-Based Waste Identification: Images of waste are processed using Google Gemini AI, classifying items into 11 predefined categories (Plastic, Paper, Organic, etc.).
  • 🦾 SCARA Robotic Sorting: AI directs robotic arm movement to accurately sort waste.
  • 📈 Hybrid Optimization Strategy:
    • 🧬 Genetic Algorithm (GA) for global search.
    • ⚙️ L-BFGS-B for fine-tuning robotic movement.
  • 📊 Data-Driven Insights: Uses Graph Convolutional Networks (GCN-LSTM) to predict waste accumulation trends and optimize collection strategies.
